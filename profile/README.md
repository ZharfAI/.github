## What we build

**Zharfban · ژرف‌بان**
Accounting and operations in one web platform for companies that keep their books in Persian.
Vouchers, cheques, bank reconciliation, payroll, warehouse and project sites live in the same
ledger, and the AI works inside the books: it reads payment slips, matches invoices and cheques,
and suggests the next line of a voucher as you type. It runs in our cloud or on the customer's own
server. [zharfban.com](https://zharfban.com)

**AI projects for businesses**
Persian assistants, document extraction, finance and accounting operations, and the data and
reporting layers behind them. Each engagement starts from one defined workflow, with a baseline and
acceptance criteria agreed before the build. [zharf.ai](https://zharf.ai)
