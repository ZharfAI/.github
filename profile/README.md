## What we build

**Zharfban · ژرف‌بان**
Accounting and operations in one web platform for companies that keep their books in Persian.
Vouchers, cheques, bank reconciliation, payroll, warehouse and project sites live in the same
ledger, and the AI works inside the books: it reads payment slips, matches invoices and cheques,
and suggests the next line of a voucher as you type. It runs in our cloud or on the customer's own
server. [zharfban.com](https://zharfban.com)

**Zharfahan · ژرف‌آهن**
A steel price reference for the Iranian market: live prices from every major mill, a complete daily
archive rather than only today's number, an assistant that answers from that data with the figure
and its date attached, and an export layer for Iraq, Afghanistan, Turkey and the GCC in four
languages. [zharfahan.com](https://zharfahan.com)

**AI infrastructure**
Servers for running language models on your own premises: NVIDIA and AMD GPU servers, Mac Studio
clusters and Chinese accelerators such as Huawei Ascend, supplied and installed with open-weight
models and a Persian assistant. Or GPU servers by the hour in Europe, the UK and the US, at live
prices. [zharf.ai/en/services/ai-infrastructure](https://zharf.ai/en/services/ai-infrastructure)

**AI projects for businesses**
Persian assistants, document extraction, finance and accounting operations, and the data and
reporting layers behind them. Each engagement starts from one defined workflow, with a baseline and
acceptance criteria agreed before the build. [zharf.ai](https://zharf.ai)
